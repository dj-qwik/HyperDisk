HOW TO PLAY

  • Knock every rival off their podium. Each fall costs a life (the pips by your name). Last runner standing wins.
  • You carry 3 discs. A thrown disc bounces off the walls and ceiling up to 3 times, then flies back to your hand. You can't throw when all 3 are out.
  • Where you hit matters. A headshot hits hardest, a chest hit is solid, and a glancing blow to the arms or legs barely moves them.

Only two things put you down: getting shoved off the edge, or the ring under your feet shattering.

  • Every hit wears you down. It shoves you toward the edge and charges your OVERLOAD, so the next hit shoves you even further. The first hit staggers you; keep eating them and one sends you flying.
  • Watch your OVERLOAD bar. The higher it climbs, the closer you are to going over. Get back to the middle of your podium after a hit to buy yourself room.
  • Losing a life resets your OVERLOAD. Headshots charge it fastest; glancing blows to arms and legs barely move the needle.
  • Shoved just past the rim, you teeter (WHOA!): push back toward the centre fast to save yourself.
  • Shattered floor: a disc that hits one of your rings makes it flash for a moment, then it drops away. Step off a flashing ring. The centre plate never breaks.
  • As in the original arcade game, a light blue line runs round all four walls at your throwing height, with a blue crosshair on it. Turn to slide the crosshair left and right; move the line up and down for elevation. The disc flies straight at the crosshair.
  • Put the crosshair on a side wall and the disc banks off it, angle in = angle out. That's how you get round a deflector or a barrier.
  • Raise the line past the top of the walls and it runs out across the ceiling: the disc bounces off the ceiling and comes down on them.
  • Lower the line to throw low and smash the rings at their feet.
  • The crosshair turns white when the throw is lined up to hit someone.
  • Shield: hold the throw button and you raise a disc over your head; a shield opens from it and bounces discs away. Tap to throw, hold to shield. You need at least one disc in hand to shield, it only covers your front, and you get 7 blocks per life. Knock a rival out to refill them.
  • Smash their disc out of the air with yours: both are destroyed and you get yours straight back.
  • Missed discs come back off the far wall and can hit you from behind, where the deflector can't help.
  • You can't walk off a platform. To cross to another of your platforms, or over a gap where a ring shattered, hold toward it at the edge for a quarter second and you leap over. Nobody can hit you mid-leap.
  • Recall calls all your discs home at once.

Power-ups appear on the podiums now and then. Walk over one to take it; it works straight away. Up to 2 are out at once, and each vanishes after 20 seconds (it blinks when it's about to go).

  • SPLIT DISC Your next 2 discs split into three as they leave your podium. Each piece hits a bit softer.
  • EXPLOSIVE Next 3 discs explode for 1.5× knockback. A shield blocks it, but the blast still shoves.
  • OVERCLOCK Next 2 discs burn straight through a shield and knock it offline for 3 s.
  • SEEKER Your next disc hunts the rival you face. It blinks and beeps so they can raise a shield in time.
  • ANCHOR For 10 s you take half the knockback and OVERLOAD.
  • SHIELD CELL +3 shield blocks.

Picking up a new disc power-up replaces the one you're carrying. Your active power-ups show next to your name. Turn them off in the lobby under POWER-UPS.
